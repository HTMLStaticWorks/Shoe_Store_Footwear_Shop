SINCE 1998

Our Humble Beginnings

Founded in a small workshop in Florence, Italy, SOLE began with a simple mission: to create formal business shoes that do not compromise orthotic health. Our founder, a master cordwainer, believed that style should feel like walking on air.

Today, we are a global luxury footwear house, maintaining artisanal hubs in Tuscany and design laboratories in New York. We craft casual sneakers, heavy outdoor boots, and sports trainers.

Our Mission

To innovate footwear ergonomics, blending century-old hand-stitched welt standards with eco-friendly cushioning to deliver elite products that respect human feet and the environment.

Our Vision

To lead global luxury fashion in setting sustainable shoe standards, assuring that every single pair uses fully traceable hides and recycled vulcanized polymers by 2030.

How to Measure Your Feet

Step 1: Tape a piece of paper flat to the floor against a solid wall.

Step 2: Stand on the paper with your heel firmly touching the wall.

Step 3: Draw a line at the longest part of your foot (usually your big toe).

Step 4: Use a ruler to measure the distance in centimeters, then look up your size on our conversion charts.

Fit Recommendation: If between sizes, order a half-size larger.
